The Bread

Our sourdough bread is made with the finest ingredients and follows a family tradition. Each loaf undergoes long fermentation with natural starter and a touch of baker's yeast, without additives or preservatives, resulting in a delicious and nutritious bread.

Each loaf is handcrafted by a baker who devotes time and passion to create a product of singular quality. Enjoy the true flavor of artisanal, organic sourdough bread!
